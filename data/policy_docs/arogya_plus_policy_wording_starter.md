## Section A — Coverage
A.1 In-patient hospitalisation: medical expenses for hospitalisation of minimum 24 consecutive hours for an illness or injury contracted during the policy period, on the advice of a medical practitioner, are payable up to the sum insured.
A.2 Day care treatment: listed day care procedures requiring less than 24 hours of hospitalisation due to technological advancement (for example cataract surgery, chemotherapy, dialysis, lithotripsy) are payable; the 24-hour condition does not apply.
A.3 Pre-hospitalisation expenses for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days are payable if related to the same condition and the in-patient claim is admissible.

## Section B — Room rent and associated expenses
B.1 Room rent, boarding and nursing is payable up to 1% of sum insured per day. ICU charges are payable up to 2% of sum insured per day.
B.2 Proportionate deduction: if the insured opts for a room category higher than eligible, all associated medical expenses (doctor/surgeon fees, OT charges, nursing) are payable in the same proportion as eligible room rent bears to actual room rent. Proportionate deduction does NOT apply to pharmacy, consumables, implants, medical devices and diagnostics, and does not apply in ICU, and does not apply where the hospital does not follow differential billing by room category.
B.3 Co-payment: Senior Shield carries a mandatory 20% co-payment on every admissible claim. Arogya Plus has no co-payment.

## Section C — Waiting periods
C.1 Initial waiting period: expenses for any illness within 30 days from the first policy commencement date are excluded, except claims arising out of an accident. Not applicable on continuous renewal.
C.2 Pre-existing diseases (PED): expenses related to declared and accepted pre-existing diseases are excluded until the expiry of 36 months of continuous coverage after the first policy inception date.
C.3 Specific disease waiting period: expenses related to cataract, hernia, joint replacement (unless due to accident), kidney/gall bladder stones and benign prostatic hyperplasia are excluded until 24 months of continuous coverage.
C.4 Moratorium: after completion of 60 continuous months of coverage, no claim shall be contestable except for proven fraud and permanent exclusions specified in the policy contract.

## Section D — Permanent exclusions
Excl. 08 Cosmetic or plastic surgery: expenses for cosmetic or plastic surgery or any treatment to change appearance unless for reconstruction following an accident, burn or cancer, or as part of medically necessary treatment certified by the attending medical practitioner.
Excl. 10 Unproven treatments: expenses for any treatment or procedure not supported by acceptable clinical evidence.
Excl. 12 Dental: dental treatment unless requiring hospitalisation due to an accident.
Excl. 15 Refractive error: spectacles, contact lenses, hearing aids and correction of refractive error below 7.5 dioptres.
Excl. 17 Sterility and infertility: expenses related to sterility, infertility, assisted reproduction (IVF, ZIFT, GIFT, ICSI), surrogacy and reversal of sterilisation.
Non-medical expenses: items listed in the list of non-payable items (IRDAI List I), such as admission kits, attendant charges, gloves, masks, toiletries, telephone, laundry and documentation charges, are not payable unless the optional consumables cover is opted.

## Section E — Claims procedure
E.1 Cashless: available only at network hospitals. Pre-authorisation request to be sent by the hospital; the insurer shall decide on the cashless request within 1 hour and on the final discharge authorisation within 3 hours of receipt of the request.
E.2 Intimation: in an emergency, intimate the claim within 24 hours of admission; for planned hospitalisation, at least 48 hours before admission.
E.3 Reimbursement: submit the claim form, original discharge summary, itemised final bill, payment receipts, investigation reports and prescriptions within 30 days of discharge. Delay may be condoned where the insured shows reasons beyond their control; a claim shall not be rejected solely on grounds of delayed intimation or submission.
E.4 The insurer shall settle or reject the claim within 30 days of receipt of the last necessary document. Where investigation is warranted it shall be completed and the claim settled within the timeline under the regulations; delay attracts interest at 2% above bank rate.
E.5 Grievance: contact the Grievance Redressal Officer of the insurer; if not resolved within 14 days (or if dissatisfied), approach Bima Bharosa (IRDAI) or the Insurance Ombudsman.

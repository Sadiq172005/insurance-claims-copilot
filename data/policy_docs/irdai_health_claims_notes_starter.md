# IRDAI health insurance — claims notes (SYNTHETIC summary, demo only)
Replace with official documents from irdai.gov.in: Master Circular on Health Insurance Business (29 May 2024), IRDAI (Insurance Products) Regulations 2024, IRDAI (Protection of Policyholders' Interests and Allied Matters of Insurers) Regulations 2024, Guidelines on Standardisation of Exclusions, List of non-payable items.

Cashless: insurers to decide on cashless authorisation requests within one hour and grant final discharge authorisation within three hours of the hospital's request; additional hospital charges caused by delay beyond three hours are borne by the insurer.
Documents: policyholders should not be asked to submit documents that the hospital or insurer can collect directly; insurer/TPA should collect required documents from the hospital.
PED: maximum waiting period for pre-existing diseases is 36 months. Specific disease waiting period maximum 36 months. Moratorium period is 60 months of continuous coverage after which claims cannot be contested except for established fraud and permanent exclusions.
Claim rejection: every repudiation must be reviewed by a claims review committee and communicated in writing with specific policy clause reasons and grievance details.
Proportionate deduction: not applicable on pharmacy, consumables, implants, medical devices and diagnostics; not applicable on ICU charges; not applicable where differential billing by room category is not followed by the hospital.
Non-payable items: IRDAI has standardised List I (items not payable) and Lists II-IV (items subsumed into room charges, procedure charges or treatment costs).
Delay: insurers must not reject genuine claims solely on the ground of delayed intimation or document submission if the delay is justified.
Fraud: insurers maintain a fraud monitoring framework; suspected fraud cases are referred to the Special Investigation Unit (SIU) and investigation must be time-bound.
Grievance: Bima Bharosa portal and Insurance Ombudsman are escalation channels for policyholders.
